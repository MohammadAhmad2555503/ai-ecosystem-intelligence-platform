8. Academic interpretation
- Topic labels are generated from top TF-IDF/NMF terms using transparent rules.
- Topic probabilities are relative NMF weights normalised per paper.
- Ambiguous topic assignments are retained but flagged for cautious interpretation.
- Metadata-noise topics are detected and handled through reassignment to the next strongest non-noise topic.
- The model uses only title and abstract text, avoiding contamination from earlier keyword theme labels.
- No metadata was invented during topic modelling.
